# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: being respectful of differing viewpoints,
giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, publishing
others' private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Instances of abusive or
unacceptable behavior may be reported through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or by contacting the
maintainer via their GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
